The **Basemap** panel controls what the map shows beneath your flights: the base map itself (**Satellite**, **Map** or **Topo**), and the overlay layers (flight lines, obstructions, airports, ground control points GCP, aeronautical). You can also add a data source of your choice.

## Open the Basemap panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the map">
    In the vertical bar on the right side of the screen, click the map icon. Its tooltip reads **Map / Sources**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the panel">
    The **Basemap** panel opens on the right. It has three areas: the choice of base map, the **Layers** list, and the **Add a source** button at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the base map

Three base maps are offered at the top of the panel. Click a tile to activate it. The selected base map is highlighted in green.

| Base map | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Satellite** | See an aerial image. Check the real terrain, buildings and vegetation. |
| **Map** | See a road map. Locate roads and place names. |
| **Topo** | See a topographic map. Read the relief. |

Changing the base map does not change your flights or your tasks. It only changes the reference image.

## Show and adjust the layers

The **Layers** list contains five layers. Each one has the same controls:

- **The eye** to the left of the name: click it to show or hide the layer. A crossed-out eye means the layer is hidden.
- **The slider** under the name: it sets the layer's opacity, from 0% (invisible) to **100%** (opaque).

| Layer | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Flight lines** | The flight lines calculated for your tasks. |
| **Obstructions** | The obstacles you have drawn or imported. |
| **Airports** | The airports shown on the map. |
| **GCP** | The ground control points (GCP). |
| **Aeronautical** | The aeronautical base layer. |

<Tip>
Lower the opacity of a layer to see the map beneath it more clearly. Hide **Flight lines** to see the base map without the flight lines.
</Tip>

## Add a source

The **Add a source** button lets you overlay your own data on the base map. Two types of source are accepted:

- an **orthophoto GeoTIFF**: a georeferenced aerial image you already have;
- a **WMS / WMTS feed**: an online map service.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the window">
    In the **Basemap** panel, click **Add a source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    The **Add a source** window opens, with the text **GeoTIFF orthophoto or WMS / WMTS feed, overlaid on the basemap.** Click **Choisir le fichier…** and select your orthophoto.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Add a source window">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/ajouter-source-carte.png" alt="Add a source window with the Cancel and Choisir le fichier… buttons" />
</Frame>

To cancel without adding anything, click **Cancel**.
