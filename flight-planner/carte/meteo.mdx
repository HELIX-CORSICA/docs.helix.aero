The **Weather** screen gathers the information you need before flying: daylight hours, and the latest METAR and TAF for the airport concerned. You can look at the active flight, or search for any airport by its ICAO code.

## Open the weather screen

1. Find the row of flights at the top of the mission, above the map.
2. Click the **cloud and sun** icon, to the left of the **Import** button. Its tooltip reads **Weather**.
3. The **Weather** screen opens for the selected flight.

To go back to the mission, click the **×** at the top right of the screen.

## Read the screen

**The top bar** shows the title **Weather** and the name of the flight concerned. The **Refresh** button reloads the data.

**The selection bar** contains:

- the green button with the name of the active flight;
- the search field **ICAO (e.g. LFPG) or "lat, lon"…**;
- the **Departure (ICAO)** field;
- the **Arrival (ICAO)** field;
- the **Flight date** field.

**The daylight window** shows, for the date you chose:

- **Sunrise**;
- **Sunset**;
- **Solar noon**;
- **Daylight duration**, in hours and minutes.

Times are given in UTC. The current UTC time appears on the right of the flight name.

**The METAR and TAF blocks** show the latest report received for the airport. If no report is available, the screen says so, for example **No METAR data for the active flight.**

The sources are listed at the bottom of the screen: METAR and TAF come from AVWX, and airport data comes from OpenAIP.

## Search for an airport

1. In the **ICAO (e.g. LFPG) or "lat, lon"…** field, type the ICAO code of the airport, for example **LFKC**.
2. Or type a position in the form "latitude, longitude".
3. Choose the result in the list. If nothing matches, the screen shows **No results.**

While the search is running, the field shows **Searching…**.

## Departure and arrival

The **Departure (ICAO)** and **Arrival (ICAO)** fields let you show the weather at the departure and arrival airports:

1. Enter the ICAO code of the departure airport in **Departure (ICAO)**.
2. Enter the ICAO code of the arrival airport in **Arrival (ICAO)**.

## Choose the flight date

The **Flight date** field sets the day analysed. Change it to see the daylight hours and reports for another day.

The text below the window tells you what is available for the date you chose:

- **METAR filtered to the UTC day; current TAF applies.** For today.
- **METAR unavailable (future day); current TAF shown.** For a future day.
- **Day's METAR filtered; current TAF not representative.** For a past day.
- **No data available (date beyond the TAF horizon).** If the date is too far away.

<Tip>
Refresh the screen before departure. Weather reports change regularly, and the **Refresh** button fetches the latest ones.
</Tip>
