The measurement tools let you check a distance, an area or the terrain directly on the map, without leaving the mission. They are at the top right of the map.

## Use a measurement tool

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the tool">
    Click the icon of the tool you want in the measurement toolbar: **Distance**, **Area**, **Altitude** or **Elevation profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure on the map">
    For **Distance**, **Area** and **Altitude**, click the map to place the points of the measurement. The result appears as you go. **Elevation profile** shows or hides with a single click on its icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the tool off">
    Click the icon of the active tool again. The map goes back to normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The four tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Distance** | Measures the length of a line you draw point by point on the map. |
| **Area** | Measures the area of a zone bounded by the points you place. |
| **Altitude** | Shows the terrain altitude at the place you click. |
| **Elevation profile** | Shows or hides the elevation profile. |

## Good to know

- **One tool at a time.** Choosing **Distance**, **Area** or **Altitude** turns off the previous tool.
- **Measurements are not kept.** When you turn the tool off or switch to another one, the measurement disappears. Note the result if you need it.
- **Unavailable during the simulation.** **Distance**, **Area** and **Altitude** are greyed out while the simulation is displayed; the tooltip then reads "Unavailable during simulation". **Elevation profile** stays available.

## The status bar

The bar at the bottom of the screen always shows reference values:

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Zoom** | The map's zoom level. |
| **View** | The area of the map currently visible. |
| **UTM** | The UTM zone and its EPSG code. |
| **Lat** / **Lon** | The coordinates of the point under the cursor. |
| **AMSL** | The altitude above mean sea level. |
| **HAE** | The altitude above the ellipsoid. |
| **Area** | The area of the drawn zone. |
