A mission groups the flights, tasks, and settings for one job. You create it from the dashboard, in two steps: the name, then the place (optional).

## Create a mission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    On the **Missions** tab, click **+ New mission**, at the top right.

    <Frame caption="The dashboard, Missions tab">
      <img src="/images/flight-planner/dashboard.png" alt="Dashboard, Missions tab, with the DJI Cloud, Import a mission and + New mission buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 01: Information">
    The creation window opens at step **01 Information**, with the subtitle "Name your mission."

    Type the name in the **Mission name** field. While the field is empty, the **Next** button stays grayed out.

    <Frame caption="Step 01 Information, empty field">
      <img src="/images/flight-planner/nouvelle-mission.png" alt="New mission window at step 01 Information, Mission name field empty" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Step 01 Information, name entered">
      <img src="/images/flight-planner/nouvelle-mission-nom.png" alt="Mission name field filled in with Corse - Haute-Corse lot 3, Next button active" />
    </Frame>

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 02: Place (optional)">
    The **02 Place** step pre-centers the map on your work area. The **Place — optional** field accepts a town or coordinates (for example `41.92 8.74`).

    <Frame caption="Step 02 Place, empty field">
      <img src="/images/flight-planner/nouvelle-mission-lieu.png" alt="Step 02 Place with the optional Place field empty, Previous and Create mission buttons" />
    </Frame>

    Start typing the name of the place. A list of suggestions appears under the field, with the country or region of each result.

    <Frame caption="Searching for Corte, with suggestions">
      <img src="/images/flight-planner/nouvelle-mission-lieu-saisi.png" alt="Place field with the text Corte and the suggestion list: Corte Corsica France, Corte Veneto Italy, and others" />
    </Frame>

    Click the suggestion you want. The field then shows the selected place.

    <Frame caption="Place selected">
      <img src="/images/flight-planner/nouvelle-mission-lieu-choisi.png" alt="Place field showing Corte, Corsica, France" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      The place is optional. To create the mission without one, click **Create mission** without typing anything in the field.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the mission">
    Click **Create mission** to confirm. To cancel at any step, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Window buttons

| Button | Step | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Cancel** | 01 and 02 | Closes the window without creating a mission. |
| **Next** | 01 | Goes to step 02 Place. Grayed out while the name is empty. |
| **Previous** | 02 | Goes back to step 01 Information. |
| **Create mission** | 02 | Creates the mission with the name and, if entered, the place. |

You can close the window at any time with the **×** button, at the top right. In that case, nothing is created.

## Import an existing mission

To reopen a mission you have already prepared, click **Import a mission**, next to **+ New mission**, on the dashboard.
