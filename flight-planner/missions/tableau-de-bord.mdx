The dashboard is the first screen you see after you sign in. It lists your missions and gives you access to the sensor and aircraft catalogs. This is where you create a new mission or import one.

## The three tabs

At the top of the screen, three tabs let you switch views:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Missions** | Your saved missions. |
| **Sensors** | The catalog of sensors available for your flight plans. |
| **Aircraft** | The catalog of airplanes, helicopters, and drones. |

## Missions tab

### Search and sort

- The **Search a mission…** field filters the list by mission name.
- The two icons to the right of the field switch the display between grid view and list view.
- The **Updated** menu sorts the missions. The **Name** option sorts them alphabetically.

### Buttons

| Button | Use |
|---|---|
| **+ New mission** | Opens the mission creation wizard. See [Create a mission](/flight-planner/missions/creer-une-mission). |
| **Import a mission** | Imports an existing flight plan. See [Import data](/flight-planner/import-export/importer). |
| **DJI Cloud** | Gives access to the missions linked to the DJI Cloud service. See [Working with DJI](/flight-planner/import-export/dji). |

### Empty list

As long as no mission has been created, the screen shows **No saved mission.** Create a mission with **+ New mission**, or import one with **Import a mission**.

<Tip>
For a first step-by-step try, follow [Your first mission in 10 minutes](/flight-planner/demarrage/premiere-mission).
</Tip>

### Rename and delete a mission

Each mission in the list can be renamed or deleted from its menu.

- **Rename** opens the **Rename mission** window, with the **Mission name** field. Confirm with **Save**.
- **Delete** asks for confirmation: **Delete the mission “name”?**
- **Lock** and **Unlock** are also in this menu.

## Sensors tab

<Frame caption="The dashboard, Sensors tab">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/dashboard-capteurs.png" alt="Sensors tab: catalog in grid view, All brands and All types filters, Sync button and + New sensor button" />
</Frame>

- The **Search a sensor…** field finds a sensor by name.
- The **All brands** and **All types** menus filter the catalog.
- Each sensor shows its type, for example **Photo** or **LiDAR**. A **3D** badge appears on the sensors that support it.
- The star at the top right of a card marks a sensor as a favorite.
- **Sync** fetches the latest catalog updates.
- **+ New sensor** adds a sensor to the catalog.

## Aircraft tab

<Frame caption="The dashboard, Aircraft tab">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/dashboard-appareils.png" alt="Aircraft tab: catalog of airplanes, helicopters and drones, with the same filters and the + New aircraft button" />
</Frame>

- The **Search an aircraft…** field finds an aircraft by name.
- The **All brands** and **All types** menus filter the catalog. The types are **Airplane**, **Helicopter**, and **Drone**.
- The star at the top right of a card marks an aircraft as a favorite.
- **Sync** fetches the latest catalog updates.
- **+ New aircraft** adds an aircraft to the catalog.
