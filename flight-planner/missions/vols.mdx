A mission groups one or more **flights**. Each flight has its own tasks (areas, corridors, points) and its own aircraft parameters. This page explains how to manage flights from the top bar of the mission.

<Frame caption="An open mission: the flight tab, the + VOL button and the Tasks panel">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/mission-vue-generale.png" alt="Open mission with the corte-lot3 flight tab, the + VOL button and the Tasks panel" />
</Frame>

## Flight tabs

Flights appear as tabs in the top bar, below the **HELIX** logo. Each tab has a number and a color, and shows the flight name. The active flight is the one whose tab is selected: the **Tasks** panel and the tasks drawn on the map apply to it.

To switch the active flight, click its tab.

## Add a flight

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + VOL">
    The **+ VOL** button sits just after the last tab. Its tooltip reads **Add a flight**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw in the new flight">
    The new flight becomes active. Then draw your tasks: they will be attached to this flight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
In the same mission, each flight can have its own tasks and its own aircraft. To split an area into several flights, see [Split an area](/flight-planner/missions/decoupe).
</Tip>

## Rename a flight

Double-click the tab name. The tooltip shows **Double-click to rename**. Type the new name.

## Hide and show a flight

The eye icon to the right of the tab name hides or shows the flight on the map. The flight stays in the mission; only its display on the map changes.

To act on all flights at once, use the tooltips **Hide all flights** and **Show all flights**.

{/* CAPTURE À FAIRE : top bar with a hidden flight (crossed-out eye) and the "Hide this flight" tooltip */}

## Flight menu

Right-click a flight tab to open its menu. It offers:

| Menu entry | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Flight settings…** | Opens the flight parameters window (see below). |
| **Duplicate flight** | Duplicates the flight. |
| **Paste flight attributes…** | Copies the attributes of another flight onto this one, after confirmation. |
| **Delete flight** | Deletes the flight and its tasks. If several flights are selected, the entry shows their number (for example **Delete 2 flights**). |

<Warning>
**Delete flight** removes the flight and the tasks it contains. Check the tab before deleting, and hide the flight rather than deleting it if you think you may still need it.
</Warning>

{/* CAPTURE À FAIRE : right-click on a flight tab, menu open */}

### Paste flight attributes

Right-click the tab of the flight to adjust, then choose **Paste flight attributes…**. The window shows a summary "Copy attributes from … to …" with the source and target flight names: check it before confirming.

{/* CAPTURE À FAIRE : "Paste flight attributes" window with the source-to-target summary */}

## Flight parameters

The **Flight parameters** window groups the settings specific to the flight. Open it with **Flight settings…** in the flight menu.

<Frame caption="Flight parameters window">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/parametres-du-vol.png" alt="Flight parameters window with Aircraft, Startup speed, Altitude offset, Enforce, Rangefinder and Turn anticipation" />
</Frame>

| Field | What to know |
|---|---|
| **AIRCRAFT** (required) | Click the **Choose an aircraft…** field to open the catalog. See below. |
| **Startup speed** | Value in m/s (3.0 by default in the screenshot). |
| **Altitude offset** | Value in meters (0.0 in the screenshot). |
| **Enforce** | Check box. Until it is checked, the field on the right is greyed out. The text under the box reads: "Off: the operator’s DJI remote RTH preset is kept." |
| **Rangefinder (real-time terrain follow)** | Check box. It shows **Not available on this aircraft** if the selected aircraft does not support it. |
| **Turn anticipation** | Value in meters (20.0 in the screenshot). |

The **Cancel** and **Confirm** buttons close the window. **Confirm** is only enabled once an aircraft is set.

### Choose the aircraft

Click the **Choose an aircraft…** field to open **Choose an aircraft**.

<Frame caption="Choose an aircraft window">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/choix-appareil.png" alt="Choose an aircraft window with the search field and the All, Airplane, Heli and Drone filters" />
</Frame>

- Type in **Search an aircraft…** to filter the list.
- The filters **All**, **Airplane**, **Heli** and **Drone** restrict the list to one aircraft type, with the number of aircraft of each type.
- Each row shows the type, the cruise speed and the flight time.
- Click the star on the right of an aircraft to add it to your favorites.
- **Cancel** closes the window without changing anything.

<Note>
You can also set the aircraft from the **Aircraft** tab of the dashboard. See [The dashboard](/flight-planner/missions/tableau-de-bord).
</Note>

## Settings specific to the task, not the flight

The altitude mode, altitude, GSD, speed and turn settings are set in the **Tasks** panel, per task, not in the **Flight parameters** window. See [Altitude, GSD and speed](/flight-planner/parametres/onglet-vol).
