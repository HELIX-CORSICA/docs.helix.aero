The **Manual multi-flight split** tool cuts an area into several pieces using lines that you draw yourself. Each piece becomes a flight. Use it when an area is too large for a single flight.

## Split an area step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the flight">
    At the top of the screen, click the tab of the flight that contains the area to split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the split tool">
    In the vertical toolbar, click the scissors icon. The tooltip shows **Manual multi-flight split**. Shortcut: the **S** key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw the cut lines">
    Click on the map to place the points of the line. Double-click to finish the line. Each line must cross the area from one side to the other: its first and last points are outside the area. To cut more than once, draw several lines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the cut">
    A bar appears above the map with the text **Split: N lines drawn** (for example **Split: 2 lines drawn**). Click **Cut**, or press the **Enter** key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Each piece becomes a flight, with its own bands. The **Split completed** notification shows the number of flights created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Correct or cancel

- **Right-click**: removes the last point placed, or the last finished line.
- **Cancel** in the **Split: N line(s) drawn** bar, or the **Esc** key: stops the current split.

## No valid cut

If no line crosses the area, the cut is refused and the message **No valid cut** appears. The details explain the problem, for example **The first point of the line is inside the AOI** or **The lines do not cut the AOI**. Draw a line that enters the area and comes out again, then click **Cut** again.

{/* CAPTURE À FAIRE : message Aucune coupe valide après une ligne qui ne traverse pas la zone */}

<Note>
If the area's trajectory is frozen (wayline), the tool shows **Split unavailable: frozen trajectory (wayline) — restore the area to split the AOI**.
</Note>
