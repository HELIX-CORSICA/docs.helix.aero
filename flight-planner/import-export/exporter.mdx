Once the strips are computed, the **Export** button turns your mission into files you can use in the field or in other software. You choose everything in the **Export the flight plan** window.

## Open the export

<Steps>
<Step title="Check the computation">
The flight strips must be computed for your area. Otherwise, the application shows "Compute the mission before exporting.".
</Step>
<Step title="Click Export">
The **Export** button is at the top right, in the mission bar.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a destination">
The window offers two options: **Flight plan** (a file to save) and **Send to DJI Pilot**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Export the flight plan window">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/export.png" alt="Export the flight plan window with the Flight plan and Send to DJI Pilot options" />
</Frame>

Click **Cancel** to close the window without exporting anything.

## Flight plan: the formats

The **Flight plan** option saves a file. The **Flight plan format** window opens. Choose the format based on what you want to do with it:

| Format | What it is | What it is for in the field |
|---|---|---|
| **.helix (Helix Archive)** | Complete **Flight Planner project** | Save the mission and share it. You can re-import it into Flight Planner and get back the exact options and strips of locked areas. |
| **KML** | Route shown on a map. **KML mode** offers **LineString** (a line) or **Placemark** (points). | View the route in mapping software, for example Google Earth, to review or present it. |
| **WPML (DJI Pilot)** | Flight plan in DJI format (KMZ file) | Prepare the mission of a DJI drone in DJI Pilot. |

<Note>
The **WPML (DJI Pilot)** format is reserved for DJI drones. If no aircraft is selected, the application shows "Select a DJI drone to enable this export.".
</Note>

## Send to DJI Pilot

This option sends the mission directly to the **DJI Pilot** app, without a file to carry around.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Send to DJI Pilot">
The line reads: "Choose the altitude reference then Local or Cloud".
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the altitude reference">
Select **Absolute AMSL** or **Relative to takeoff**. Pick the option that matches how you want altitudes to be read in DJI Pilot.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Local or Cloud">

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Local** | Sends the mission to DJI Pilot on the remote controller, over the local network. |
| **Cloud** | Publishes the mission to the Helix portal. DJI Pilot then retrieves it online. |

</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
This option is only available for a DJI drone. With any other aircraft, it is disabled and shows "Non-DJI aircraft — reserved for DJI drones".
</Warning>

## If a problem is detected on the flight

Before export, the application may show "Major issues were detected on your flight.", for example **Terrain collision detected!**. Fix the flight first if you can. To export anyway, tick "I understand the risks and still want to export." then click **Continue anyway**.
