For a DJI drone, you can send the flight plan directly to **DJI Pilot**, either locally or through the **Cloud**. You can also browse the missions of your DJI account from the dashboard.

## Before you send

- The mission must be **computed**. Otherwise, you see the message *"Compute the mission before exporting."*
- The flight must use a **DJI aircraft**. For a non-DJI aircraft, **Send** is greyed out with the message *"Non-DJI aircraft — reserved for DJI drones"*.

## Send to DJI Pilot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export">
    Click **Export** at the top right of the mission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the DJI destination">
    Click **Send to DJI Pilot**. The subtitle reads: *"Choose the altitude reference then Local or Cloud"*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the altitude reference">
    In **Altitude reference (DJI WPML)**, choose:
    - **Absolute AMSL**: altitudes are expressed above sea level.
    - **Relative to takeoff**: altitudes are expressed relative to the takeoff point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Local or Cloud">
    - **Local**: *"LAN bridge to DJI Pilot on the same remote controller"*. The plan arrives in DJI Pilot on the remote controller connected to your local network.
    - **Cloud**: *"Publishes waylines on the Helix portal for DJI Pilot"*. The plan is published to the portal, then picked up by DJI Pilot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Confirm with **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Local or Cloud: what is the difference?

| Option | When to choose it | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| **Local** | You are in the field, with the DJI remote controller on the same network as this computer. | The DJI Pilot bridge must be started on this computer. |
| **Cloud** | You want to publish the plan to the Helix portal and find it from DJI Pilot. | A license that allows DJI cloud publishing. |

If your license does not allow it, Helix shows: *"Your license does not allow DJI cloud publishing."*

### The DJI Pilot bridge (Local)

For a **Local** send, the bridge window shows **Start** and **Stop**, the **Address** to enter in DJI Pilot, a 6-digit **Code**, and the detected **Remote controller**.

{/* CAPTURE TO DO: DJI Pilot bridge window (Start / Stop buttons, Address, Code, Remote controller) */}

In DJI Pilot, the address is entered under **Cloud Service → Open Platforms**, and then the local password is requested.

## DJI account missions (DJI Cloud)

The **DJI Cloud** button on the dashboard opens the list of missions in your DJI account.

If no mission is found, you see the message *"No mission from this account is on the DJI server."*

## Waylines

The DJI Pilot bridge window has a **Waylines** section. Waylines are the flight plans as DJI Pilot stores them in its library.

{/* CAPTURE TO DO: Waylines list in the DJI Pilot bridge window */}

## WPML export for DJI Pilot (file)

To save a file instead of sending it, open **Export**, then **Flight plan**, and choose **WPML (DJI Pilot)**. This option is only enabled when a DJI drone is selected. Otherwise, you see *"Select a DJI drone to enable this export."*
