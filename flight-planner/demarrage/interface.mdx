A mission opens on a map surrounded by panels and control bars. This page walks through the
mission screen area by area, so you know where each control is before you start.

The sections below go through the screen from top to bottom:

1. The top bar
2. The flight row
3. The Tasks panel
4. The drawing toolbar
5. The measurement tools
6. The basemap and quality control
7. The compass and the Duration / Distance box
8. The status bar

## 1. The top bar

At the very top of the screen, from left to right:

- **Logo and home button**: returns you to the dashboard.
- **Bell**: shows notifications. A red number indicates how many notifications are waiting.
- **Sync indicator**: shows the state of synchronization. It displays **Sync error** when synchronization has failed.
- **Gear icon**: opens **Settings**.
- **Online**: indicates that the software is connected.

## 2. The flight row

Below the top bar, a mission contains one or more flights. Each flight has its own tab.

- **Numbered tabs**: each flight appears with its number and name. Click a tab to open that flight.
- **Eye icon**: to the right of the tab, hides the flight on the map. The flight stays in the mission.
- **+ FLIGHT**: adds a flight to the mission. See [Organize flights](/flight-planner/missions/vols).
- **Import**: menu that offers **Zone**, **GCP** and **Obstructions**. See [Import data](/flight-planner/import-export/importer).

<Frame caption="Import menu">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/menu-import.png" alt="Import menu open with the entries Zone, GCP and Obstructions" />
</Frame>

- **Export**: green button on the right. It opens the flight plan export. See [Export the flight plan](/flight-planner/import-export/exporter).

## 3. The Tasks panel

The **Tasks** panel, on the left of the map, lists the tasks of the selected flight.

- **Tasks**: the numbered list of tasks. Click a task to select it.
- **Collapse arrow** (`<`): at the top right of the panel, collapses the panel to free up the map.
- **STRIPS**: collapsed list of flight strips, with their count. It appears once the strips have been calculated. Click it to expand the list and see each strip with its altitude and length. See [Flight strips](/flight-planner/parametres/bandes).
- **FLIGHT**, **SENSOR**, **GEOMETRY**: three tabs that group the settings of the task.
  - **FLIGHT**: altitude mode, altitude, GSD and speed. See [Altitude, GSD and speed](/flight-planner/parametres/onglet-vol).
  - **SENSOR**: sensor, gimbal pitch and trigger. See [Choose the sensor](/flight-planner/parametres/onglet-capteur).
  - **GEOMETRY**: overlap and orientation. See [Overlap and orientation](/flight-planner/parametres/onglet-geometrie).

## 4. The drawing toolbar

The vertical bar on the left edge of the map groups the drawing tools. Hover over an icon to see its name. From top to bottom:

1. **Takeoff point**
2. **Place a waypoint**
3. **Draw area**
4. **Draw corridor**
5. **Draw obstruction**
6. **Manual multi-flight split**

<Frame caption="Tooltip of the Draw area tool">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/barre-outils-dessin.png" alt="Vertical drawing toolbar with the Draw area tooltip" />
</Frame>

Activating a tool deactivates the previous one. See [Draw tasks](/flight-planner/missions/taches) and [Split an area](/flight-planner/missions/decoupe).

## 5. The measurement tools

At the top right of the map, four tools let you measure directly on the map: **Distance**, **Area**, **Altitude** and **Elevation profile**. Hover over an icon to see its name. See [Measure on the map](/flight-planner/carte/mesures).

## 6. The basemap and quality control

On the right of the map, two buttons open panels:

- **Basemap**: chooses the background (**Satellite**, **Map** or **Topo**) and shows or hides the layers (**Flight lines**, **Obstructions**, **Airports**, **GCP**, **Aeronautical**). See [Basemaps and layers](/flight-planner/carte/fonds-et-couches).
- **Quality control** (QC): opens the quality control panel. See [Quality control (QC)](/flight-planner/qualite/controle-qualite).

## 7. The compass and the Duration / Distance box

- **Compass**: at the bottom right of the map, it shows north.
- **Duration / Distance**: box to the left of the compass. It shows the duration and distance of the flight plan once the strips have been calculated.

<Frame caption="Calculated strips and the Duration / Distance box">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/mission-bandes-calculees.png" alt="Calculated strips over the area, Duration and Distance box at the bottom right, collapsed STRIPS list, CAPTEUR tab open" />

## 8. The status bar

The bar at the very bottom of the screen always shows:

- **Zoom**: the zoom level of the map.
- **View**: the extent visible on the screen.
- **UTM**: the zone and coordinate system.
- **Lat** and **Lon**: latitude and longitude.
- **AMSL** and **HAE**: altitudes.
- **Area**: the area of the zone.

## Moving around the map with the mouse

- **Mouse wheel**: zoom in and out.
- **Hold the left button and drag** on an empty area: moves the map.
