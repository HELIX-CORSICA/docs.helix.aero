Flight Planner asks for a **Helix Aero** account when it starts. Once you are signed in, you can create and sync your missions.

## Open the application

1. Launch **Helix Flight Planner**.
2. The **Sign in** screen appears.
3. Enter your **Email address**, then your **Password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

## The fields on the Sign in screen

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | The address of your Helix Aero account. |
| **Password** | The password for this account. Click the eye icon to show or hide it (**Show password** / **Hide password**). |
| **Remember me** | Checkbox, checked by default. |
| **Sign in** | Starts the sign-in. |

<Note>
By signing in, you agree to the Helix terms of use.
</Note>

## License required

An account is not enough: you need an **active license** to plan missions. If the account has no valid license, the application shows **License required** and offers **Manage my license**.

You can check the license type and its status in **Settings → License**. See [Settings](/flight-planner/reglages).

<Warning>
To activate a new computer, sign in online at least once. The machine is registered on your license at that time.
</Warning>

## Online or offline

At the top right of the window, an indicator shows the connection status:

- **Online**: the application is connected to your Helix Aero account.
- **Offline**: the connection is lost. Your changes are queued, and the message shows how many there are (**Offline — n operation(s) queued**).

Next to it, the sync icon shows whether your missions are synced.

## Sign out

1. Open **Settings**, then the **Account** tab.
2. Click **Sign out**.

See [Settings](/flight-planner/reglages) for details on each tab.
