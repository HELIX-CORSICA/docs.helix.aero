The **SENSOR** tab of the **Tasks** panel defines the camera used for a task: which sensor it is, how the camera triggers, and at what interval. The flight strips are calculated from these settings.

## Open the tab

1. Open a mission, then the flight concerned.
2. Select the task on the map or in the **Tasks** list.
3. In the **Tasks** panel, click the **SENSOR** tab.

## Choose the sensor

1. Click the **Sensor** field (it shows **Choose a sensor…** while it is empty).
2. The **Choose a sensor** window opens.
3. Click the sensor you want: it appears in the **Sensor** field.

<Frame caption="The Choose a sensor window, with the filters and favorites">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/choix-capteur.png" alt="Choose a sensor window with the search field, the All, Photo and LiDAR filters, and the favorite stars" />
</Frame>

In this window:

- **Search**: type part of the sensor name in the **Search a sensor…** field. The list narrows as you type.

<Frame caption="Searching a sensor by name">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/choix-capteur-recherche.png" alt="Choose a sensor window with the search Matrice 4E and three results" />
</Frame>

- **Filters**: **All**, **Photo** or **LiDAR**. Each filter shows how many sensors it contains.
- **Favorites**: click the star on the right of a sensor to mark it as a favorite.
- **Cancel**: closes the window without changing the sensor.

## Set the capture parameters

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gimbal pitch** | Camera angle in degrees. The value shown in the example is **-90** (camera pointing straight down). |
| **Trigger** | How photos are triggered: **Distance** or **Time**. |
| **Min interval (s)** | Minimum interval between two photos, in seconds. The field appears once a sensor is chosen. |
| **Capture in turns** | Toggle: turns photo capture on or off during turns. |

### Trigger

- **Distance**: a photo is triggered at regular intervals along the path (one every X m).
- **Time**: a photo is triggered at regular time intervals (one every X s).

### Min interval

Below the **Min interval (s)** field, the **Range** allowed for the chosen sensor is shown, for example **Range 0.5–2 s**. Enter a value within that range.

### Capture in turns

Click the **Capture in turns** toggle to turn it on or off.

<Warning>
Without a sensor, no strips are calculated. The message **"Choose a sensor to compute the strips."** stays displayed as long as the **Sensor** field is empty.
</Warning>

## Once the sensor is chosen

When the sensor and parameters are filled in, the flight strips appear over the task area. The **Duration / Distance** block at the bottom right of the map gives an estimate of the flight time and the length of the path.
