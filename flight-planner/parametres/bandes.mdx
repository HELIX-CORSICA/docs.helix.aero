The **strips** are the flight lines the aircraft will follow to cover the area of a task. They appear on the map as soon as the calculation is done, together with the total flight duration and distance.

## The result on the map

Once the calculation is complete, the area is covered with **green strips**. Each strip carries **direction arrows** that show the way the aircraft will fly.

<Frame caption="Strips calculated over the area, with the Duration / Distance panel at the bottom right">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/mission-bandes-calculees.png" alt="Map with an area covered by green strips with direction arrows, the STRIPS list collapsed in the Tasks panel, and the Duration and Distance panel at the bottom right" />
</Frame>

The strips recalculate automatically when you change a task setting or the shape of the area. You don't need to run anything again.

## The STRIPS list

Under the selected task, the **Tasks** panel shows a **STRIPS** line with the number of calculated strips. Click the arrow to the left of **STRIPS** to expand the list.

Each line is one strip, identified by a letter and a number (**A1**, **A2**, **A3**…). It shows:

- the **altitude range** of the strip, for example `120→120 m`;
- the **length** of the strip, on the right, for example `864 m` or `1.1 km`.

<Frame caption="Expanded STRIPS list: each strip shows its altitude and its length">
  <img src="/images/flight-planner/liste-bandes.png" alt="Expanded STRIPS list with strips A1 to A8, each showing its altitude range and its length" />
</Frame>

## Duration and Distance

The panel at the bottom right of the map shows the total for the calculated flight:

- **Duration**: the estimated flight time, for example `30 min 42 s`;
- **Distance**: the total distance to fly, for example `20 km`.

These two values change each time the strips are recalculated.
